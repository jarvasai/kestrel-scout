# Community Code of Conduct

KESTREL welcomes students, hobbyists, educators, and engineers across experience levels. We want a constructive, safe, and inclusive project space.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and specific; assume good intent while remaining open to correction.
- Explain technical disagreement with evidence, measurements, and alternatives.
- Credit other people's code, CAD, photography, documentation, and ideas.
- Share safety concerns promptly, especially around batteries, high-current servo power, and moving linkages.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sustained personal attacks, deliberate misinformation about testing or funding, and unsafe instructions presented as verified are not acceptable.

## Project response

Project maintainers may edit or remove harmful material, close discussions, or restrict participation to protect the community. To report a concern, use the repository's GitHub reporting tools or privately contact the project owner after the public repository is created. Do not post private safety or personal information publicly.
